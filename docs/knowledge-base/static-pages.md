# Static Pages

## What is Static Pages?

Static Pages are the public-facing pages of the Human Essentials platform that anyone can see without logging in. This includes the main landing page (homepage), the privacy policy, and the help/FAQ page for logged-in users.

## Key Capabilities

- Public landing page introducing Human Essentials with features, testimonials, and a registration link
- Privacy policy page accessible to everyone
- Help/FAQ page with searchable questions and answers (requires login)
- Automatic redirect: logged-in users visiting the homepage are sent to their appropriate dashboard
- FAQ content is managed by super admins through the Admin Panel

## How It Works

1. When someone visits the Human Essentials website without being logged in, they see the public landing page.
2. The landing page describes what the platform does, highlights key features, shows testimonials, and provides a link to request an account.
3. Visitors can click "Login" to sign in to their existing account.
4. The Privacy Policy page is accessible from the footer and explains how data is handled.
5. Once logged in, users who visit the homepage are automatically redirected to their dashboard (organization dashboard, partner dashboard, or admin dashboard depending on their role).
6. The Help page (accessible after login) shows a searchable list of frequently asked questions. Type a keyword to filter questions and find relevant answers.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I customize the landing page?**
A: The landing page content is part of the platform and is not customizable by individual organizations. Contact platform administrators for changes.

**Q: Who manages the FAQ content on the Help page?**
A: Super admins create and manage FAQ questions and answers through the Admin Panel. These appear on the Help page for all logged-in users.

**Q: Why am I redirected to my dashboard when I visit the homepage?**
A: If you are already logged in, the system sends you directly to your dashboard since the landing page is designed for visitors who are not yet signed in.

**Q: Is the privacy policy always available?**
A: Yes. The privacy policy page is accessible without logging in and can be found in the footer of the landing page.

**Q: How do I search the FAQ?**
A: On the Help page, type a keyword in the search box. The FAQ list filters automatically to show matching questions.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Share the landing page link with prospective organizations that might want to join the platform.
- Check the Help/FAQ page before contacting support -- your question may already be answered.
- Super admins should keep FAQ content up to date to reduce support requests.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- The landing page cannot be customized by individual organizations.
- The Help/FAQ page requires a login to access.
- FAQ content is managed exclusively by super admins.

## Related Features

- **Account Requests** -- The landing page links to the account registration form
- **Users & Authentication** -- The login button on the landing page leads to the sign-in form
- **Admin Panel** -- Super admins manage FAQ questions displayed on the Help page
- **Dashboard** -- Logged-in users are redirected from the homepage to their dashboard
