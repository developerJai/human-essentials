# Organizations

## What is Organizations?

An organization in Human Essentials represents your essentials bank (or diaper bank). It is the central entity that everything else connects to -- your inventory, partners, donations, distributions, users, and settings all belong to your organization. The Organizations feature lets admins configure how their essentials bank operates on the platform.

## Key Capabilities

- Configure organization details: name, contact information, address, and logo
- Manage partner-related settings like approval workflows and request types
- Set default storage locations for new transactions
- Invite and manage users within your organization
- Promote users to admin or demote admins to regular users
- Control which types of partner requests are enabled (quantity-based, individual, child-based)
- Configure email notification preferences
- Set up distribution reminders and printout preferences

## How It Works

1. Organization admins navigate to the "My Organization" or settings area.
2. From there, they can update the organization's profile information (name, address, logo, contact details).
3. Admins can configure partner settings, such as which request types partners can use and whether the organization receives email copies of partner requests.
4. User management is also done here -- inviting new users, removing users, and changing user roles.
5. Distribution settings like reminder schedules and printout options can be configured.
6. Changes take effect immediately for all users in the organization.

## Common Questions

**Q: Who can change organization settings?**
A: Only organization admins can modify settings. Regular users can view some settings but cannot change them.

**Q: Can I have multiple organizations?**
A: Each essentials bank is a separate organization. Users can belong to multiple organizations and switch between them, but each organization has its own data, inventory, and partners.

**Q: How do I invite a new user to my organization?**
A: Go to the user management section, enter the new user's email address, and send an invitation. They will receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

**Q: What is the difference between an admin and a regular user?**
A: Admins can manage organization settings, invite and remove users, promote/demote users, and delete transactions. Regular users can perform day-to-day operations like recording donations and creating distributions but cannot change settings or manage users.

**Q: Can I change my organization's name?**
A: Yes. Organization admins can update the name and other profile details at any time.

**Q: What are the partner request type settings?**
A: You can enable or disable three types of partner requests: quantity-based (partners request specific quantities), individual (partners request for specific individuals), and child-based (partners request based on children served). Enable only the types your partners use.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep your organization profile complete and up to date, especially your logo and contact information -- these appear on printed documents like packing slips.
- Set up at least two admin users so organization management is not dependent on one person.
- Configure partner request types to match your actual workflow to avoid confusion.
- Review user access periodically and remove users who have left the organization.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Only organization admins can change settings. Regular users cannot.
- Removing a user from the organization does not delete their historical activity (donations, distributions, etc.).
- Some settings (like request types) affect what partners see in their portal, so communicate changes to your partners.

## Related Features

- **Users & Authentication** -- User accounts and roles are managed within the organization context
- **Partners** -- Partner relationships and settings are configured per organization
- **Storage Locations** -- Default storage locations are set at the organization level
- **Distributions** -- Distribution reminders and printout settings are organization-level configurations
- **Admin Panel** -- Super admins can manage all organizations from the admin panel
