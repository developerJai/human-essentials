# Reports

## What is Reports?

Reports provide summaries and detailed breakdowns of your organization's activity. There are two types: date-range summary reports for day-to-day operational visibility, and NDBN Annual Reports that compile year-end statistics required by the National Diaper Bank Network.

## Key Capabilities

- Date-range summary reports for donations, distributions, purchases, product drives, and manufacturer donations
- Itemized breakdown reports showing per-item detail with on-hand quantities
- Activity graph comparing received donations, purchases, and distributions side by side
- NDBN Annual Reports covering eight categories: Diapers, Warehouse/Storage, Adult Incontinence, Period Supplies, Other Products, Partner Info, Children Served, and Year-End Summary
- Annual reports are saved and cached -- they load quickly and can be recalculated on demand
- Export annual reports to CSV for sharing with the NDBN or your board
- Filter summary reports by date range

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Reports from the main menu.
2. For day-to-day reports:
   - Select the report type (Donations Summary, Distributions Summary, Purchases Summary, etc.)
   - Choose a date range using the date picker
   - View the summary with totals, recent transactions, and breakdowns
3. For annual reports:
   - Navigate to Annual Reports
   - Select the year you want to view
   - The system displays pre-computed statistics across all eight report categories
   - If data has changed, click "Recalculate Report" to refresh the numbers
   - Export to CSV for external use
4. The Activity Graph shows a visual comparison of donations received, items purchased, and items distributed over your selected date range.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the NDBN Annual Report?**
A: The National Diaper Bank Network (NDBN) requires member organizations to submit annual statistics. The Annual Report feature automatically computes these numbers from your data across eight categories, saving hours of manual data gathering.

**Q: Why does my annual report show old data?**
A: Annual reports are cached for performance. If you have entered new data since the report was last calculated, click "Recalculate Report" to refresh the numbers.

**Q: Can I get a report for the current year?**
A: Annual reports are only available for completed years. For current-year data, use the date-range summary reports.

**Q: What does the itemized breakdown report show?**
A: It lists each item type with the total quantity donated, distributed, or requested during the selected period, along with current on-hand quantities. Items running low are highlighted.

**Q: Can I export reports?**
A: Annual reports can be exported to CSV. Date-range summary reports are viewable on screen.

**Q: What is the activity graph?**
A: The activity graph is a bar chart comparing three metrics over your selected date range: total donations received, total items purchased, and total items distributed. It helps you quickly see the balance between incoming and outgoing supplies.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Run annual reports at the start of each year for the previous year and export them before data changes.
- Use the date-range reports regularly to monitor operational metrics and spot trends.
- Share the activity graph with your board to provide a visual overview of operations.
- Recalculate annual reports if you have made corrections to historical data.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Annual reports are only available for completed years, not the current year.
- Annual report data is cached -- click "Recalculate" after making corrections to historical transactions.
- Date-range summary reports cannot be exported to CSV (use annual reports for exportable data).
- Report accuracy depends on consistent data entry -- items must have correct categories and reporting categories for totals to be meaningful.

## Related Features

- **Donations** -- Donation data feeds into donation summaries and annual reports
- **Distributions** -- Distribution data drives distribution summaries and most annual metrics
- **Purchases** -- Purchase data feeds into purchase summaries and spending breakdowns
- **Product Drives** -- Drive data appears in the product drives summary report
- **Partners** -- Partner data contributes to the annual Partner Info report
- **Items** -- Item categories and reporting categories determine how data is grouped in reports
- **Historical Trends** -- Separate feature showing 12-month trend charts
