# Partner Portal

## What is the Partner Portal?

The Partner Portal is a self-service area where your partner agencies log in to manage their own information and request supplies. Instead of calling or emailing your essentials bank, partners can submit requests, update their profiles, manage the families and children they serve, and view their distribution history -- all online.

## Key Capabilities

- Submit supply requests (quantity-based, individual-based, or child-based depending on organization settings)
- View past requests and their current status (pending, started, fulfilled, cancelled)
- View distribution history showing what supplies have been received
- Manage family and children records for child-based request tracking
- Update partner profile information (contact details, service area, agency type, etc.)
- Receive email notifications about request confirmations and cancellations
- Multiple users per partner agency can have their own login

## How It Works

1. A partner user logs in using their email and password (provided via invitation).
2. They land on the Partner Dashboard, which shows recent activity and any pending requests.
3. To request supplies, the partner clicks "New Request" and selects the type:
   - **Quantity Request**: Request specific quantities of items (for example, 200 diapers size 3)
   - **Individual Request**: Request items for specific individuals served
   - **Child Request**: Request items based on children in their care
4. The partner fills in the items and quantities, adds any comments, and submits.
5. The request appears on the essentials bank's request queue for review and fulfillment.
6. The partner receives an email confirmation that their request was received.
7. Once the bank fulfills the request by creating a distribution, the partner can see it in their distribution history.
8. If the bank cancels a request, the partner receives an email notification with the reason.

## Common Questions

**Q: How does a partner get access to the portal?**
A: The essentials bank invites the partner and its users. Partners receive an email invitation with a link to set up their password and log in.

**Q: Can a partner have multiple users?**
A: Yes. A partner agency can have several users, each with their own login. This is useful when different staff members handle different tasks.

**Q: What request types are available?**
A: This depends on the essentials bank's settings. Banks can enable quantity-based requests, individual requests, child-based requests, or any combination.

**Q: Can partners see their distribution history?**
A: Yes. The portal shows a history of all distributions the partner has received, including dates and item details.

**Q: Can partners manage family and children information?**
A: Yes. For organizations that use child-based requests, partners can add, edit, and manage the families and children they serve. This information is used when submitting child-based requests.

**Q: What happens after a partner submits a request?**
A: The request goes to the essentials bank's queue. Bank staff review, approve, and fulfill the request by creating a distribution. The partner is notified at each step.

**Q: Can partners edit a request after submitting it?**
A: No. Once a request is submitted, the partner cannot edit it. If changes are needed, the partner should contact the essentials bank directly.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Submit requests well in advance of when supplies are needed to allow processing time.
- Keep profile information up to date, especially contact details and mailing address.
- Check the portal regularly for updates on request status and distribution schedules.
- If your organization uses child-based requests, keep family and children records current for accurate requests.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Partners can only see their own requests and distributions, not those of other partners.
- Requests cannot be edited after submission -- partners must contact the bank for changes.
- Available request types depend on the essentials bank's configuration.
- Partners cannot directly modify their inventory -- all supplies come through the bank's distribution process.

## Related Features

- **Partners** -- Bank-side management of partner agencies
- **Requests** -- Partner requests appear on the bank's request queue
- **Distributions** -- Fulfilled requests become distributions visible to partners
- **Partner Groups** -- Groups control which items partners can request
- **Organizations** -- Request type settings are configured at the organization level
