# Partners

## What is Partners?

Partners are the agencies and organizations that receive supplies from your essentials bank. They might be shelters, food pantries, family service organizations, or other nonprofits that serve families in need. The Partners feature lets you manage the full lifecycle of partner relationships -- from initial setup through ongoing collaboration.

## Key Capabilities

- Create and manage partner agency profiles
- Invite partners to join the platform and access the Partner Portal
- Approve or reject partner applications
- Assign partners to partner groups for shared settings
- Track partner status: invited, awaiting review, approved, recertification required, deactivated
- Set up recertification requirements to periodically verify partner information
- Deactivate partners who are no longer active
- View partner request and distribution history
- Set default storage locations per partner
- Configure partner quotas (maximum request amounts)

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Partners from the main menu.
2. Click "New Partner" to add a partner agency.
3. Enter the partner's name, email, and basic information.
4. Save and invite the partner. They receive an email with a link to complete their profile and set up their account.
5. Once the partner completes their profile, they appear as "Awaiting Review" in your partner list.
6. Review their profile and approve them. Approved partners can now submit supply requests through the Partner Portal.
7. Over time, you can require recertification -- partners update their information to confirm they still meet your requirements.
8. If a partner becomes inactive, deactivate them. They can be reactivated later if needed.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the partner approval process?**
A: After you invite a partner and they complete their profile, their status changes to "Awaiting Review." You review their information and approve or reject them. Only approved partners can submit supply requests.

**Q: What is recertification?**
A: Recertification is a periodic process where partners update their profile information to confirm they still meet your requirements. You set the recertification schedule, and partners are notified when it is time to recertify.

**Q: Can I deactivate a partner?**
A: Yes. Deactivating a partner prevents them from submitting new requests and logging into the portal. Their historical data (past requests and distributions) is preserved.

**Q: What is a partner quota?**
A: A quota is an optional limit on how many items a partner can request. When set, the system highlights when a request approaches or exceeds the quota.

**Q: Can I assign a default storage location to a partner?**
A: Yes. Setting a default storage location means distributions to that partner will default to pulling items from that location.

**Q: How do partner groups work with individual partners?**
A: Assigning a partner to a group applies the group's item restrictions and reminder schedule to that partner. See the Partner Groups feature for details.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Set clear expectations with partners during the approval process about request procedures and timelines.
- Use recertification to keep partner information current and verify ongoing eligibility.
- Assign partners to groups to efficiently manage item access and reminders.
- Deactivate rather than delete inactive partners to preserve their history.
- Set quotas for partners that have specific allocation limits.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Partners cannot be deleted if they have historical requests or distributions -- use deactivation instead.
- Each partner can belong to only one partner group.
- Deactivated partners cannot log in or submit requests.
- Email notifications are not sent to deactivated partners.

## Related Features

- **Partner Portal** -- Where partners log in to manage their profile and submit requests
- **Partner Groups** -- Groups apply shared settings (item restrictions, reminders) to partners
- **Requests** -- Partners submit supply requests through the portal
- **Distributions** -- Supplies sent to partners are recorded as distributions
- **Organizations** -- Partner settings and approval workflows are configured at the organization level
