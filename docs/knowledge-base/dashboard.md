# Dashboard

## What is the Dashboard?

The Dashboard is the home page you see after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your organization's current status, including outstanding partner requests, low inventory alerts, recent activity, and important announcements.

## Key Capabilities

- View outstanding partner requests that need attention
- See partners awaiting approval
- Get alerts for items running low in inventory
- View recent donations, distributions, and purchases at a glance
- Read broadcast announcements from your organization or the platform
- Quick access to common actions like creating distributions or recording donations

## How It Works

1. Log in to Human Essentials.
2. You are automatically taken to the Dashboard.
3. The dashboard displays several information panels:
   - **Outstanding Requests** showing partner supply requests waiting to be fulfilled
   - **Partner Approvals** listing partners waiting for your approval
   - **Low Inventory Alerts** highlighting items that have fallen below recommended levels
   - **Recent Activity** summarizing the latest donations, distributions, and other transactions
   - **Announcements** displaying any messages from your admin or the platform
4. Click on any item or section to go to its detail page and take action.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I customize what appears on my dashboard?**
A: No. The dashboard layout is the same for all users in an organization. It automatically shows the most relevant information based on current data.

**Q: What do the low inventory alerts mean?**
A: These alerts appear when the quantity of an item at any storage location falls below the minimum or recommended level you have set for that item.

**Q: How often is the dashboard updated?**
A: The dashboard shows current data each time you load or refresh the page.

**Q: I do not see any announcements. Is that normal?**
A: Yes. Announcements only appear when an admin has posted one. If there are no active announcements, that section will be empty.

**Q: Can partners see this dashboard?**
A: No. Partners have their own separate dashboard in the Partner Portal. The main dashboard is for essentials bank staff only.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Check the dashboard daily to stay on top of pending requests and low inventory.
- Use the low inventory alerts to proactively reach out to donors or plan purchases.
- Respond to outstanding partner requests promptly to maintain good partner relationships.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- The dashboard is not customizable -- all users in an organization see the same layout.
- Partners and super admins have their own separate dashboards with different information.
- Low inventory alerts only show up if minimum or recommended quantities have been configured for items.

## Related Features

- **Requests** -- Outstanding partner requests appear on the dashboard
- **Partners** -- Partner approval notifications appear on the dashboard
- **Broadcast Announcements** -- Active announcements are shown on the dashboard
- **Items** -- Low inventory alerts are based on item quantity thresholds
- **Storage Locations** -- Inventory levels are tracked per storage location
