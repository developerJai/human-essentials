# Purchases

## What is Purchases?

Purchases track supplies that your essentials bank buys from vendors using its own funds. Each purchase records what was bought, how much was spent, which vendor supplied the goods, and where the items are stored. Purchases are tracked separately from donations so you can accurately report your organization's spending.

## Key Capabilities

- Record purchases with detailed item lists and quantities
- Track spending broken down by four product categories: diapers, adult incontinence, period supplies, and other
- Select the vendor and storage location for each purchase
- Filter and search past purchases by vendor, storage location, item category, or date range
- Export purchase data to CSV
- View running totals for quantity, dollar amount, and fair market value
- Automatic inventory updates when a purchase is recorded

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Purchases and click "New Purchase."
2. Select the vendor you purchased from. (If the vendor is not listed, you can create one on the spot.)
3. Choose the storage location where the purchased items will be stored.
4. Enter the total amount spent.
5. Optionally, break down spending by category (diapers, adult incontinence, period supplies, other). If provided, the category amounts must add up to the total.
6. Add the individual items and quantities purchased.
7. Set the purchase date.
8. Save the purchase.
9. The system automatically adds the purchased items to the selected storage location's inventory.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the difference between a purchase and a donation?**
A: A purchase involves spending money to buy supplies from a vendor. A donation is supplies received for free from a donor (manufacturer, drive, donation site, or other source). They are tracked separately for financial reporting.

**Q: Why do I need to break down spending by category?**
A: The category breakdown (diapers, adult incontinence, period supplies, other) is used for reporting, particularly the NDBN annual report. It is optional -- if you leave all categories at zero, the breakdown is skipped.

**Q: Can I edit a purchase after recording it?**
A: Yes, but editing may be restricted if an inventory snapshot has been taken since the purchase was recorded.

**Q: Can I delete a purchase?**
A: Only organization admins can delete purchases, and only if no inventory snapshot has occurred since the purchase was created.

**Q: Does recording a purchase update inventory automatically?**
A: Yes. When you save a purchase, the items and quantities are immediately added to the selected storage location's inventory.

**Q: Can I create a vendor while recording a purchase?**
A: Yes. If the vendor is not in your list, you can create one directly from the purchase form without leaving the page.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Enter the category breakdown when possible -- it makes your annual reports more accurate.
- Record purchases promptly to keep inventory counts current.
- Use the filter and export features for financial reconciliation and budget tracking.
- Link purchases to the correct vendor for accurate spending-per-vendor reports.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Category amounts (if provided) must add up exactly to the total amount spent.
- Purchases tied to inventory snapshots cannot be edited or deleted.
- Only organization admins can delete purchases.
- Each purchase is linked to one vendor and one storage location.

## Related Features
