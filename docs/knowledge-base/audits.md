# Audits

## What is Audits?

Audits let you perform a formal physical inventory count at a storage location and reconcile the results with what the system shows. Unlike quick adjustments, audits follow a multi-step process to ensure accuracy before finalizing changes.

## Key Capabilities

- Create a new audit for any storage location to verify actual inventory
- Multi-step workflow: In Progress, Confirmed, then Finalized
- Compare system quantities against physical counts before finalizing
- Automatically creates adjustments to correct any differences upon finalization
- View audit history filtered by storage location
- Only one active (non-finalized) audit per storage location at a time

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Audits and click "New Audit."
2. Select the storage location you want to audit.
3. Enter the actual quantities you counted for each item at that location.
4. Save the audit. It starts in the "In Progress" state.
5. Review your counts. You can edit the audit while it is still in progress.
6. When you are satisfied with the counts, confirm the audit. This moves it to "Confirmed" status.
7. Finally, finalize the audit. The system compares your counts against what it has on record and automatically creates adjustments for any differences.
8. After finalization, the audit is locked and cannot be changed.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the difference between an audit and an adjustment?**
A: An audit is a formal, multi-step inventory verification process. You count everything at a location, review your counts, and finalize to apply corrections. An adjustment is a quick, one-step manual correction for individual items.

**Q: Can I have multiple audits open at the same storage location?**
A: No. Only one audit can be in progress or confirmed (not yet finalized) per storage location at a time.

**Q: Can I edit an audit after it is finalized?**
A: No. Once finalized, the audit and its results are locked permanently.

**Q: What happens when I finalize an audit?**
A: The system compares your physical counts to the recorded inventory. If there are differences, it automatically creates adjustments to correct the inventory to match your counts.

**Q: Can I cancel an audit that is in progress?**
A: You can delete an audit that has not been finalized. Once finalized, it cannot be removed.

**Q: Do audits prevent other inventory changes?**
A: Finalized audits can block the deletion of certain older transactions (like transfers or purchases) that occurred before the audit. This protects the integrity of the verified counts.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Count all items at the location before starting the audit in the system to save time.
- Have a second person verify counts for high-value or high-volume items.
- Add notes or comments to explain any significant discrepancies found.
- Conduct audits periodically (for example, quarterly) to catch errors early.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Only one active audit is allowed per storage location.
- Finalized audits are permanent and cannot be edited or deleted.
- Finalizing an audit may prevent deletion of older transactions at that location (transfers, purchases, donations) to preserve data integrity.

## Related Features

- **Adjustments** -- Audits automatically create adjustments when finalized to correct differences
- **Storage Locations** -- Each audit is tied to a specific storage location
- **Inventory Events** -- Audit-related adjustments feed into the inventory tracking system
