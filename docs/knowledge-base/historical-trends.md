# Historical Trends

## What is Historical Trends?

Historical Trends shows you monthly charts of your organization's activity over the past 12 months. You can see how donations, distributions, and purchases have changed over time, helping you spot patterns, plan ahead, and make data-driven decisions.

## Key Capabilities

- View monthly bar charts for distributions, donations, and purchases
- See 12-month trends to identify seasonal patterns
- Compare activity levels across different months
- Data is refreshed daily for up-to-date reporting

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Historical Trends from the main menu.
2. Select the type of trend you want to view: Distributions, Donations, or Purchases.
3. A bar chart displays monthly totals for the past 12 months.
4. Use the chart to identify patterns, such as higher demand during certain seasons or months.

## Common Questions

**Q: How far back does the trend data go?**
A: The charts display the most recent 12 months of data.

**Q: How often is the data updated?**
A: Trend data is refreshed once per day. Very recent transactions may not appear until the next daily refresh.

**Q: Can I export the trend data?**
A: The Historical Trends feature displays charts for visual analysis. For detailed data exports, use the Reports feature.

**Q: What do the numbers in the charts represent?**
A: The numbers represent total quantities of items donated, distributed, or purchased during each month.

**Q: Can I compare different types of trends side by side?**
A: Each trend type (distributions, donations, purchases) is displayed on its own chart. You can switch between them to compare, but they are not combined into a single chart.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Check trends regularly to anticipate seasonal demand increases (such as back-to-school or holiday periods).
- Use trend data to plan purchasing and fundraising activities.
- Share trend charts with your board or funders to demonstrate impact over time.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Trends cover only the most recent 12 months -- older data is not displayed.
- Data is updated daily, not in real time.
- Trends show quantity totals only, not dollar amounts.

## Related Features

- **Reports** -- For detailed summaries and annual reports with more granular data
- **Donations** -- Donation trends track incoming supply volumes
- **Distributions** -- Distribution trends track outgoing supply volumes
- **Purchases** -- Purchase trends track bought supply volumes
