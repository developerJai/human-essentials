# Product Drives

## What is Product Drives?

Product Drives are community donation campaigns organized by your essentials bank. A product drive is a time-limited event where individuals, businesses, or groups collect supplies on your behalf. The Product Drives feature lets you set up campaigns, track participating locations and people, and see how much was collected.

## Key Capabilities

- Create and manage product drive campaigns with start and end dates
- Add participants (individuals, businesses, or groups) to each drive
- Link donations to the product drive they came from
- View summary statistics: total items collected, number of donations, money raised
- Tag drives for easy filtering and organization
- Export drive data for reporting
- Track drive performance over time

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Product Drives from the main menu.
2. Click "New Product Drive" to create a campaign.
3. Enter the drive name, start date, end date, and any other details.
4. Optionally add participants -- the individuals, businesses, or community groups running collection points for this drive.
5. Save the drive.
6. As donations come in from this drive, record them as donations with "Product Drive" as the source and select this specific drive.
7. The drive's detail page shows a running total of items collected, number of donations received, and other summary statistics.
8. After the drive ends, review the results to assess its success.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is a product drive participant?**
A: A participant is a person, business, or group that actively collects supplies during the drive. For example, a local church running a collection box would be a participant.

**Q: How do I link a donation to a product drive?**
A: When recording a donation, select "Product Drive" as the source type and then choose the specific drive from the dropdown.

**Q: Can I see total items collected for a drive?**
A: Yes. The product drive detail page shows the total quantity of items donated, the number of individual donations, and if tracked, the monetary value.

**Q: Can I run multiple drives at the same time?**
A: Yes. You can have multiple active drives running simultaneously.

**Q: What are drive tags?**
A: Tags are labels you can assign to drives for organization and filtering. For example, you might tag drives as "Holiday," "Corporate," or "School."

**Q: Can I add participants after the drive has started?**
A: Yes. You can add or remove participants at any time during or after the drive.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Set clear start and end dates for drives to track performance within a defined period.
- Add participants early so you can track which collection points are most productive.
- Use tags to categorize drives for easier reporting and comparison.
- Review drive results to identify your most effective campaigns and replicate them.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Donations must be manually linked to a product drive when recorded -- the system does not automatically associate them.
- Product drive statistics depend on donations being properly tagged with the correct drive.
- Removing a participant from a drive does not remove the donations they collected.

## Related Features
