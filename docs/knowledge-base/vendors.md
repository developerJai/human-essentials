# Vendors

## What is Vendors?

Vendors are businesses that your essentials bank purchases supplies from. The Vendors feature lets you maintain a directory of your suppliers, track how much you have bought from each one, and manage vendor information for purchasing and reporting purposes.

## Key Capabilities

- Create and manage vendor records with business name, contact information, and address
- Track total purchase volume per vendor
- Link purchases to the vendor they were bought from
- Export vendor data to CSV with volume totals
- Import vendors in bulk from a CSV file
- Deactivate vendors you no longer purchase from (preserving purchase history)
- Reactivate previously deactivated vendors
- Create vendors on the fly while recording a purchase

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Vendors from the main menu.
2. Click "New Vendor" to add a supplier.
3. Enter the business name, contact person, phone, email, and address.
4. Save the vendor. It is now available when recording purchases.
5. When creating a purchase, select this vendor as the supplier. If a vendor is not in the list, you can create one directly from the purchase form.
6. View any vendor's detail page to see their purchase history and total volume of items bought.
7. If you stop buying from a vendor, deactivate them. They will not appear in purchase form dropdowns, but all purchase history is preserved.
8. Reactivate a vendor if you start buying from them again.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the difference between a vendor and a manufacturer?**
A: Vendors are businesses you buy from (purchases cost money). Manufacturers are companies that donate supplies for free. They are tracked separately for financial reporting.

**Q: Can I delete a vendor?**
A: You can delete a vendor only if they have no purchases linked to them. If purchases exist, use the deactivate option instead to preserve the purchase history.

**Q: Can I see how much I have bought from a vendor?**
A: Yes. The vendor list shows total volume per vendor, and the vendor detail page shows complete purchase history.

**Q: Can I create a vendor while recording a purchase?**
A: Yes. If the vendor is not in your list, you can create one directly from the purchase form without leaving the page.

**Q: Can I import vendors from a spreadsheet?**
A: Yes. You can upload a CSV file to create multiple vendor records at once.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep vendor contact information current for easy reordering and communication.
- Review purchase volumes per vendor to identify your top suppliers.
- Deactivate inactive vendors rather than deleting them to preserve purchase records.
- Use the CSV export to share vendor lists with your finance team.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Vendors with existing purchases cannot be deleted -- use deactivation instead.
- Deleting a vendor that has no purchases permanently removes the record.
- Vendor records are specific to your organization and not shared between organizations.
- Deactivated vendors do not appear in dropdown menus for new purchases.
