# Manufacturers

## What is Manufacturers?

Manufacturers are companies that donate supplies to your essentials bank. The Manufacturers feature lets you maintain a directory of these corporate donors and track the volume of goods each one has contributed over time.

## Key Capabilities

- Create and manage a list of manufacturer donors
- Record contact information and address for each manufacturer
- Link donations to the manufacturer they came from
- Track total donation volume per manufacturer
- Export manufacturer data to CSV
- Import manufacturers in bulk from a CSV file
- Deactivate manufacturers that are no longer active donors

## How It Works

1. Navigate to Manufacturers from the main menu.
2. Click "New Manufacturer" to add a company to your directory.
3. Enter the manufacturer's name, contact person, phone, email, and address.
4. Save the manufacturer. It is now available as a source when recording donations.
5. When recording a new donation, select "Manufacturer" as the source type and choose the specific manufacturer.
6. View any manufacturer's detail page to see their donation history and total contribution volume.

## Common Questions

**Q: How do manufacturers differ from vendors?**
A: Manufacturers donate supplies for free. Vendors are businesses you purchase supplies from. They are tracked separately because donations and purchases have different financial implications.

**Q: Can I see how much a manufacturer has donated?**
A: Yes. Each manufacturer's detail page shows their donation history and total volume of items contributed.

**Q: Can I deactivate a manufacturer?**
A: Yes. If a manufacturer is no longer donating, you can deactivate them. They will not appear in dropdown lists for new donations, but their historical donation records are preserved.

**Q: Can I import manufacturers from a spreadsheet?**
A: Yes. You can upload a CSV file to create multiple manufacturer records at once.

**Q: Can I delete a manufacturer?**
A: You can delete a manufacturer only if they have no donations linked to them. Otherwise, use the deactivate option.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep manufacturer contact information up to date for relationship management.
- Review manufacturer donation volumes periodically to recognize top contributors.
- Deactivate inactive manufacturers rather than deleting them to preserve donation history.

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Manufacturers with existing donation records cannot be deleted -- use deactivation instead.
- Manufacturer records are specific to your organization and are not shared across the platform.
