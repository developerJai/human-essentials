# Reports User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization user or admin.
- Your organization must have some transaction data (donations, distributions, purchases) to generate meaningful reports.

## Overview

Reports provide date-range summaries and analytics about your organization's inventory activity. Human Essentials offers multiple report types including activity graphs, donation summaries, distribution summaries, purchase summaries, product drive summaries, itemized reports, trend analyses, distributions by county, and the NDBN Annual Survey. Use reports to analyze operations, prepare grant applications, and complete the NDBN annual reporting requirements.

---

## Task 1: Access the Reports Menu

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** to expand the menu.
2. You will see the following report options:
   - **Activity Graph**
   - **Annual Survey**
   - **History**
   - **Distributions - Summary**
   - **Distributions - By County**
   - **Distributions - Itemized**
   - **Distributions - Trends**
   - **Donations - Summary**
   - **Donations - Itemized**
   - **Donations - Manufacturer**
   - **Donations - Trends**
   - **Product Drives - Summary**
   - **Purchases - Summary**
   - **Purchases - Trends**
   - **Requests - Itemized**
3. Click any report name to open it.

### Expected Result

The selected report page loads with its filters and data.

---

## Task 2: View the Activity Graph

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Activity Graph**.
2. The page shows a visual graph of donations, distributions, and purchases over time.
3. Use the date range picker to adjust the time period.
4. Click **Filter** to update the graph.

### Expected Result

You see a graphical representation of your organization's inflow and outflow activity for the selected period.

---

## Task 3: Generate a Distributions Summary Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Distributions - Summary**.
2. Select a **Date Range** using the date range picker.
3. Click **Filter**.
4. The report displays a summary of all distributions within the selected period, broken down by item and partner.

### Expected Result

You see a table summarizing distribution quantities by item and partner, with totals for the selected date range.

---

## Task 4: Generate a Distributions by County Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Distributions - By County**.
2. Select a **Date Range** if desired and click **Filter**.
3. The report shows distribution totals grouped by the county of each partner agency.

### Expected Result

You see distribution volumes organized by geographic area, useful for demonstrating community impact.

---

## Task 5: View Itemized Distribution or Donation Reports

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Distributions - Itemized** or **Donations - Itemized**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and any other available filters.
3. Click **Filter**.
4. The report shows individual line items for each transaction.

### Expected Result

You see a detailed, item-by-item breakdown of every distribution or donation in the selected period.

---

## Task 6: View Donation Summary Reports

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Donations - Summary**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and click **Filter**.
3. The report shows donation totals by source type and item.

### Expected Result

You see a summary of all donations, organized by source (product drive, manufacturer, donation site, miscellaneous), for the selected period.

---

## Task 7: View Manufacturer Donation Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Donations - Manufacturer**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and click **Filter**.
3. The report shows donations grouped by manufacturer.

### Expected Result

You see how much each manufacturer has donated during the selected period.

---

## Task 8: View Product Drives Summary Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Product Drives - Summary**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and click **Filter**.
3. The report shows summary statistics for each product drive within the date range.

### Expected Result

You see total items collected, number of donations, and other statistics for each product drive.

---

## Task 9: View Purchases Summary Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Purchases - Summary**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and click **Filter**.

### Expected Result

You see a summary of all purchases within the selected period, including totals by item and cost breakdowns.

---

## Task 10: View Trend Reports

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** and select one of the trend reports:
   - **Distributions - Trends**
   - **Donations - Trends**
   - **Purchases - Trends**
2. The trend page shows monthly or periodic trends as a chart or table.

### Expected Result

You see how donation, distribution, or purchase volumes have changed over time, helping you identify seasonal patterns and long-term trends.

---

## Task 11: View Itemized Requests Report

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Requests - Itemized**.
2. Select a **Date Range** and click **Filter**.

### Expected Result

You see a detailed breakdown of all partner requests during the selected period, showing what items were requested and in what quantities.

---

## Task 12: Complete the NDBN Annual Survey

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Reports** > **Annual Survey**.
2. Select the reporting year from the available options.
3. The system auto-fills survey fields based on your organization's transaction data for that year.
4. Review the pre-filled values and make any necessary adjustments.
5. Use the report to complete your actual NDBN Annual Survey submission.

### Expected Result

The Annual Survey page displays calculated values for the selected year, covering distributions, donations, purchases, families served, and other metrics required by NDBN. This saves significant time compared to calculating values manually.

---

## Variations

- **Date range selection**: Most reports support custom date ranges. If no date range is selected, some reports may default to the current month or year.
- **Export options**: Some reports offer CSV export for further analysis in spreadsheet programs.
- **History page**: The History report (also accessible under Reports) shows the raw inventory event log, covered in the Inventory Events manual.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| A report shows no data. | There may be no transactions in the selected date range, or filters are too restrictive. | Widen the date range or clear other filters. Verify that transactions exist for the period you are reviewing. |
| Annual Survey values do not match your expectations. | Some transactions may be missing or incorrectly categorized. | Review your donation, distribution, and purchase records to ensure all transactions are recorded and categorized correctly. |
| The report page loads slowly. | The date range may span a very large period with many transactions. | Narrow the date range to reduce the amount of data being processed. |
| You cannot find a specific report type. | The report may be listed under a different name in the sidebar. | Review the full **Reports** menu in the sidebar. All report types are listed there. |
