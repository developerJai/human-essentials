# Account Requests User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must have access to the Human Essentials public website.
- You must represent an essentials bank (not a partner agency).
- You must have a valid email address for confirmation.
- If your organization is an NDBN member, have your membership details ready.

## Overview

Account Requests allow new essentials banks to request a free account on Human Essentials. You fill out a form describing your organization, confirm your email address, and then wait for a site administrator to review and approve your request. Partner agencies do not use this form; they are added by their essentials bank directly.

---

## Task 1: Submit an Account Request

### Steps

1. Navigate to the Human Essentials website and open the **Essentials Bank Account Request Form** page.
2. Select the radio button labeled **I am an Essentials Bank**.
3. The bank registration form appears below the radio buttons.
4. In the **Name** field, enter your full name.
5. In the **Email** field, enter your organization email address.
6. In the **Organization name** field, enter your essentials bank's name.
7. In the **Organization website** field, enter your organization's website URL (e.g., https://www.example.com).
8. In the **NDBN Membership** dropdown, select your NDBN member entry if applicable. Leave blank if you are not an NDBN member.
9. In the **Request Details (min 50 characters)** field, describe your organization and how you plan to use Human Essentials. You must enter at least 50 characters.
10. Complete the reCAPTCHA verification.
11. Click the **Submit** button.

### Expected Result

You are redirected to a confirmation page indicating your request has been received. A confirmation email is sent to the email address you provided.

---

## Task 2: Confirm Your Email Address

### Steps

1. Open your email inbox and find the confirmation email from Human Essentials.
2. Click the confirmation link in the email.

### Expected Result

Your browser opens a page confirming your email has been verified. Your account request is now queued for administrator review. You will receive another email when your account is approved or rejected.

---

## Task 3: Understand What Happens After Submission

### Steps

1. After confirming your email, wait for a site administrator to review your request.
2. If approved, you will receive an email with login credentials and instructions to access your new organization dashboard.
3. If rejected, you will receive an email explaining the decision.

### Expected Result

Upon approval, you can log in to Human Essentials and begin configuring your essentials bank. Upon rejection, you may contact the administrators for further information.

---

## Variations

- **Partner agencies**: If you are a partner agency, do not use this form. Your essentials bank must create your account. The form displays a message directing you to contact your local bank.
- **Staging environment**: If you are on a staging or test environment, the form will not be available. You will see a message directing you to the live Human Essentials website.
- **Non-NDBN members**: The NDBN Membership field is optional. Leave it blank if your organization is not a member.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| The registration form does not appear after selecting the radio button. | You may have selected the partner agency option instead of the essentials bank option. | Select the radio button labeled **I am an Essentials Bank** to reveal the form. |
| You receive a validation error about request details. | Your description in the **Request Details** field is fewer than 50 characters. | Enter a longer description that meets the 50-character minimum. |
| The reCAPTCHA does not load or fails. | Your browser may be blocking scripts or you have a slow connection. | Refresh the page, ensure JavaScript is enabled, and try again. |
| You did not receive a confirmation email. | The email may be in your spam or junk folder, or you entered the wrong address. | Check your spam folder. If not found, submit a new request with the correct email address. |
| The confirmation link says "invalid token." | The link may have expired or already been used. | Request a new account or contact the Human Essentials support team. |
