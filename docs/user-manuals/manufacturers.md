# Manufacturers User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization user or admin.

## Overview

Manufacturers represent companies that donate goods to your essentials bank. Managing manufacturer records lets you track which companies contribute inventory and the volume of their donations over time. Manufacturers can be created from the dedicated page or inline when recording a donation.

---

## Task 1: View All Manufacturers

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Community** to expand the menu.
2. Click **Manufacturers**.
3. The page displays a table of all manufacturers with their names and associated donation volumes.

### Expected Result

You see a list of all manufacturers for your organization with summary information about their contributions.

---

## Task 2: Create a New Manufacturer

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Manufacturers** page by clicking **Community** > **Manufacturers** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **New Manufacturer** button.
3. In the **Name** field, enter the manufacturer's name.
4. (Optional) Fill in any additional contact or address fields.
5. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The manufacturer is created and appears in the manufacturers list. It is now available as a selection when recording donations with a Manufacturer source.

---

## Task 3: Create a Manufacturer Inline During a Donation

### Steps

1. Navigate to **Donations** > **New Donation** in the left sidebar.
2. Select **Manufacturer** as the **Source**.
3. If the manufacturer you need does not appear in the **Manufacturer** dropdown, click the **Add manufacturer** link below the form.
4. In the modal that appears, enter the manufacturer's **Name** and any other details.
5. Click **Save** in the modal.
6. The new manufacturer is now available in the **Manufacturer** dropdown on the donation form.
7. Select it and continue creating the donation.

### Expected Result

The manufacturer is created without leaving the donation form and is immediately selectable.

---

## Task 4: Edit a Manufacturer

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Manufacturers** page.
2. Find the manufacturer you want to edit.
3. Click the **Edit** button in the actions column.
4. Update the **Name** or other fields as needed.
5. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The manufacturer information is updated.

---

## Task 5: Delete a Manufacturer

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Manufacturers** page.
2. Find the manufacturer you want to remove.
3. Click the **Delete** button in the actions column.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

### Expected Result

The manufacturer is removed from the system. This action may be restricted if the manufacturer has associated donations.

---

## Variations

- **Donation volume tracking**: The manufacturers list shows the total volume of donations from each manufacturer, helping you identify your largest corporate donors.
- **Inline creation**: You can create manufacturers on the fly from the donation form, which is convenient when recording a donation from a new manufacturer.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| A manufacturer does not appear in the donation form dropdown. | The manufacturer may not have been created yet. | Create the manufacturer from the **Community** > **Manufacturers** page or use the inline creation link on the donation form. |
| You cannot delete a manufacturer. | The manufacturer may have associated donation records. | If deletion is not possible due to existing records, consider renaming the manufacturer or leaving it in the list for historical accuracy. |
| The inline manufacturer creation modal does not open. | JavaScript may be blocked or your browser has a pop-up blocker interfering. | Ensure JavaScript is enabled and pop-up blockers are not preventing the modal from opening. Try refreshing the page. |
