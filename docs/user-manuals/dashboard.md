# Dashboard User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization user or admin.

## Overview

The Dashboard is the main landing page you see after logging in. It provides an at-a-glance summary of your organization's activity, including outstanding partner requests awaiting action, partners pending approval, low inventory alerts, recent donations and distributions, and any broadcast announcements. Use the dashboard to quickly identify items that need your attention.

---

## Task 1: Access the Dashboard

### Steps

1. Log in to Human Essentials with your credentials.
2. You are automatically taken to the **Dashboard** page.
3. Alternatively, click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar at any time to return to it.

### Expected Result

The Dashboard page loads, displaying summary widgets and information panels for your organization.

---

## Task 2: Review Outstanding Requests

### Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, locate the section showing outstanding partner requests.
2. Review the count of pending requests displayed.
3. Click on the requests link or the notification bell icon in the top navigation bar to go directly to the **Requests** page for more details.

### Expected Result

You see how many partner requests are currently pending. Clicking the link takes you to the full requests list where you can start fulfillment.

---

## Task 3: Review Partners Pending Approval

### Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, locate the section showing partners awaiting review.
2. Review the count of partners that need approval.
3. Click the link to navigate to the **Partner Agencies** page to review and approve them.

### Expected Result

You see the number of partner agencies in an "awaiting review" status. Clicking through takes you to the partners list where you can approve or reject them.

---

## Task 4: Check Low Inventory Alerts

### Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, locate the low inventory section.
2. Review the list of items that are below their minimum threshold.
3. Use this information to plan purchases, donations, or product drives to replenish stock.

### Expected Result

You see a list of items whose current inventory level is below the configured minimum quantity, helping you identify what needs restocking.

---

## Task 5: View Recent Activity

### Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, review the summary panels showing recent donations and distributions.
2. Note the totals and recent entries displayed.
3. Click any link or summary item to navigate to the detailed list for that activity type.

### Expected Result

You see an overview of recent donation and distribution activity for your organization, giving you a quick sense of inflows and outflows.

---

## Task 6: Read Broadcast Announcements

### Steps

1. On the **Dashboard**, locate the announcements section.
2. Read any active broadcast announcements posted by your organization admins.

### Expected Result

You see all current, non-expired broadcast announcements relevant to your organization.

---

## Task 7: Use the Top Navigation Bar

### Steps

1. At the top of the page, click the **calendar icon** to see upcoming pick-ups and deliveries for the current week.
2. Click the **bell icon** to see a dropdown with counts of pending requests and partners awaiting review, with links to each.
3. Click your **username** in the top-right to access a dropdown with **Account Settings**, options to switch between organizations (if applicable), **My Co-Workers**, **My Organization**, and **Log Out**.

### Expected Result

The top navigation bar provides quick access to your schedule, notifications, account settings, and organization management without navigating away from your current page.

---

## Variations

- **Organization admin view**: Admins see the **My Organization** link in the user dropdown and have access to the organization settings page.
- **No pending items**: If there are no outstanding requests or partners awaiting approval, those sections display zero counts.
- **Multiple organizations**: If you belong to multiple organizations, use the user dropdown to switch between them.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| The dashboard shows zero for all counts even though you expect data. | You may be viewing the wrong organization, or data has not been entered yet. | Check that you are logged in to the correct organization using the user dropdown at the top right. |
| Low inventory alerts do not appear. | Minimum inventory thresholds may not be configured for your items. | Go to **Inventory** > **Items & Inventory** and set minimum quantity thresholds for items you want to track. |
| You do not see broadcast announcements on the dashboard. | There may be no active announcements, or all announcements have expired. | Ask your organization admin to create a new announcement if needed. |
| The dashboard loads slowly. | Your organization may have a large volume of data. | Wait for the page to fully load. If the issue persists, contact your system administrator. |
