# Product Drives User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization user or admin.

## Overview

Product Drives track community donation campaigns organized by your essentials bank. A product drive has a name, start and end date, and can be associated with participants (businesses, organizations, or individuals running collection points). When recording donations, you can link them to a product drive to track how much inventory each drive generates. The system provides summary statistics for each drive.

---

## Task 1: View All Product Drives

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Community** to expand the menu.
2. Click **Product Drives**.
3. The page displays a table of all product drives with names, dates, and summary statistics.

### Expected Result

You see a list of all product drives for your organization, including both active and past drives.

---

## Task 2: Create a New Product Drive

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Product Drives** page by clicking **Community** > **Product Drives** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **New Product Drive** button.
3. In the **Name** field, enter a name for the drive (e.g., "Spring 2026 Diaper Drive").
4. In the **Start Date** field, select the drive's start date.
5. In the **End Date** field, select the drive's end date.
6. (Optional) Add any **Tags** to categorize the drive.
7. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The product drive is created and appears in the product drives list. It is now available as a selection when recording donations with a Product Drive source.

---

## Task 3: View Product Drive Details

### Steps

1. On the **Product Drives** page, find the drive you want to view.
2. Click the **View** button or the drive name.

### Expected Result

The product drive detail page shows the drive's name, dates, associated participants, linked donations, total items collected, and summary statistics.

---

## Task 4: Edit a Product Drive

### Steps

1. On the **Product Drives** page, find the drive you want to edit.
2. Click the **Edit** button.
3. Update the **Name**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, or **Tags** as needed.
4. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The product drive information is updated.

---

## Task 5: Delete a Product Drive

### Steps

1. On the **Product Drives** page, find the drive you want to remove.
2. Click the **Delete** button.
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

### Expected Result

The product drive is removed. This may be restricted if donations are linked to the drive.

---

## Task 6: Manage Product Drive Participants

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Community** > **Product Drive Participants**.
2. The page displays all participants (businesses, organizations, or individuals) that run collection points during drives.
3. To add a new participant, click the **New Product Drive Participant** button.
4. Enter the participant's **Business Name** or **Contact Name**, and any other relevant details.
5. Click **Save**.
6. To edit or delete a participant, use the corresponding buttons in the actions column.

### Expected Result

Product drive participants are managed and available for selection when recording donations linked to product drives.

---

## Task 7: Link a Donation to a Product Drive

### Steps

1. Navigate to **Donations** > **New Donation**.
2. Select **Product Drive** as the **Source**.
3. Select the specific drive from the **Product Drive** dropdown.
4. (Optional) Select the **Product Drive Participant** from the dropdown.
5. Complete the rest of the donation form and click **Save**.

### Expected Result

The donation is linked to the product drive. The drive's summary statistics update to include this donation's items and quantities.

---

## Variations

- **Tags**: Use tags to categorize drives by type, season, or sponsor for easier filtering and reporting.
- **Participants**: Product drive participants are separate from product drives. One participant can contribute to multiple drives, and one drive can have multiple participants.
- **Inline creation**: You can create new product drives and participants inline from the donation form without navigating away.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| A product drive does not appear in the donation form dropdown. | The drive may not have been created yet. | Go to **Community** > **Product Drives** and create the drive first. |
| The product drive summary shows fewer donations than expected. | Some donations may not have been linked to the drive. | Check the donation records and ensure each relevant donation has the correct product drive selected as the source. |
| You cannot delete a product drive. | The drive may have associated donation records. | Consider leaving the drive in the system for historical reference rather than deleting it. |
