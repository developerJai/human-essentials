# Organizations (My Organization) User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization admin.

## Overview

The My Organization page is the central settings hub for your essentials bank. Here you configure your organization's name, contact information, request types, email templates, partner form customization, printing preferences, and user management. Only organization admins can access and modify these settings.

---

## Task 1: Access Organization Settings

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **My Organization**.
2. Alternatively, click your **username** in the top-right corner and select **My Organization** from the dropdown.

### Expected Result

The organization settings page loads, showing your current configuration across multiple sections.

---

## Task 2: Update Organization Details

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **My Organization** page.
2. Locate the organization details section.
3. Update the **Name**, **Short Name**, **URL**, **Email**, **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip Code** fields as needed.
4. Click the **Save** button for that section.

### Expected Result

Your organization's basic information is updated. The name appears throughout the application and in partner-facing communications.

---

## Task 3: Configure Partner Request Types

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the section for request types or partner request settings.
2. Enable or disable the request types your organization supports:
   - **Quantity-based requests** - Partners request items by quantity.
   - **Individual requests** - Partners request based on individuals served.
   - **Child/family-based requests** - Partners request based on children and families in their care.
3. If you enable the packs feature (custom request units), configure the unit names for items that support pack requests.
4. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

Partners will see only the enabled request types when submitting requests through the partner portal.

---

## Task 4: Customize Email Templates

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the email templates section.
2. Edit the templates for various automated emails:
   - Distribution notification emails sent to partners.
   - Reminder emails sent before a scheduled distribution.
   - Other organization-specific email templates.
3. Use the available placeholder variables (displayed on the page) to personalize emails.
4. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

Future automated emails use your customized templates. Partners receive personalized communications matching your organization's tone and branding.

---

## Task 5: Manage Users

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the users section.
2. To invite a new user, click the **Invite User** button and enter their email address.
3. To change a user's role, use the role dropdown next to their name (options typically include Organization Admin and Organization User).
4. To remove a user, click the **Remove** button next to their name and confirm.

### Expected Result

Users are added, modified, or removed from your organization. Invited users receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

---

## Task 6: Configure Printing Preferences

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the printing and distribution settings section.
2. Configure preferences such as:
   - Whether to use barcodes in distribution PDFs.
   - Default print settings for receipts and manifests.
3. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

Future PDF generation for distributions and other documents follows your updated print preferences.

---

## Task 7: Customize Partner Profile Fields

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the partner profile or partner form customization section.
2. Enable or disable which fields partners are required to fill out in their profiles (e.g., program details, service area, number of people served).
3. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

The partner portal profile form reflects your customization. Partners see only the fields you have enabled.

---

## Task 8: Set Inventory Defaults

### Steps

1. On the **My Organization** page, locate the inventory or distribution defaults section.
2. Set a default storage location for new transactions if desired.
3. Configure any other inventory-related default settings.
4. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

New donations, distributions, and other transactions default to your selected settings, saving time during data entry.

---

## Variations

- **Organization admin only**: Only users with the Organization Admin role can access the **My Organization** page. Regular users do not see it in the sidebar.
- **Multiple admins**: You can have multiple organization admins who all have access to these settings.
- **Account settings**: Individual user preferences (password, name) are managed separately through **Account Settings** in the user dropdown, not on the organization page.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| You do not see **My Organization** in the left sidebar. | You are not logged in as an organization admin. | Ask a current admin to upgrade your role to Organization Admin. |
| An invited user says they did not receive the invitation email. | The email may have gone to spam, or the email address was entered incorrectly. | Re-send the invitation with the correct email address and ask the user to check their spam folder. |
| Partners cannot see a request type you enabled. | The change may not have been saved, or partners need to refresh their portal. | Verify the setting is saved on the **My Organization** page and ask partners to log out and back in. |
| Email template changes do not appear in sent emails. | You may not have saved the template, or cached templates are being used. | Confirm the save was successful and try sending a test email to verify the updated template. |
