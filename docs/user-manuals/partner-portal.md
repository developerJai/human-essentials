# Partner Portal User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be a partner agency user with an active account.
- Your essentials bank must have invited and approved your partner agency.
- You must have received your login credentials via the invitation email.

## Overview

The Partner Portal is the self-service interface for partner agencies. Through the portal, you can submit requests for supplies, manage family and children profiles (if applicable), view your distribution history, update your organization's profile, and read announcements from your essentials bank. The portal is separate from the bank's main interface and is designed specifically for partner agency staff.

---

## Task 1: Log In to the Partner Portal

### Steps

1. Navigate to the Human Essentials partner login page.
2. Enter your **Email** address.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Click the **Log In** button.

### Expected Result

You are taken to the Partner Portal dashboard, which shows announcements, recent distributions, and quick links to common actions.

---

## Task 2: Submit a New Request for Supplies

### Steps

1. On the Partner Portal dashboard, click the **New Request** button (or navigate to the requests section).
2. Depending on your bank's configuration, you may see one of the following request types:
   - **Quantity-based request**: Select items and enter the quantity needed for each.
   - **Individual request**: Enter the number of individuals you are requesting supplies for.
   - **Child/family-based request**: Enter details about the children and families needing supplies.
3. For a quantity-based request:
   a. Select an item from the **Item** dropdown.
   b. Enter the desired **Quantity**.
   c. Click **Add Another Item** to request additional items.
4. (Optional) Add any **Comments** or notes about the request.
5. Click the **Submit** button.

### Expected Result

Your request is submitted and set to a pending status. Your essentials bank will review the request and begin fulfillment. You can see the request status on your requests list.

---

## Task 3: View Request History

### Steps

1. In the Partner Portal, navigate to the **Requests** section.
2. The page displays a list of all your past and current requests with their statuses (Pending, Started, Fulfilled, Discarded).

### Expected Result

You see all your submitted requests and their current statuses, letting you track which requests are in progress and which have been fulfilled.

---

## Task 4: View Distribution History

### Steps

1. In the Partner Portal, navigate to the **Distributions** section.
2. The page displays a list of all distributions your agency has received, with dates, item details, and quantities.

### Expected Result

You see a record of all supplies your agency has received from the essentials bank.

---

## Task 5: Manage Families

### Steps

1. In the Partner Portal, navigate to the **Families** section.
2. To add a new family, click the **New Family** button.
3. Enter the family details including any required fields set by your bank.
4. Click **Save**.
5. To edit an existing family, click the **Edit** button next to the family record.
6. Update the information and click **Save**.

### Expected Result

Family records are created or updated. These records can be used when submitting child/family-based requests.

---

## Task 6: Manage Children Profiles

### Steps

1. In the Partner Portal, navigate to the **Children** section (or access children through the family record).
2. To add a new child, click the **New Child** button.
3. Enter the child's details (name, date of birth, item needs, etc.).
4. Click **Save**.
5. To edit an existing child, click the **Edit** button next to the child record and update the information.

### Expected Result

Children profiles are created or updated. These are used in child/family-based requests to determine the appropriate items and sizes.

---

## Task 7: Update Your Organization Profile

### Steps

1. In the Partner Portal, navigate to the **My Profile** or **Organization Profile** section.
2. Review your organization's information (address, programs served, contact info, etc.).
3. Update any fields as needed.
4. Click **Save**.

### Expected Result

Your organization's profile is updated. Your essentials bank can see the updated information. Some fields may be required by your bank for recertification.

---

## Task 8: Read Announcements

### Steps

1. On the Partner Portal dashboard, locate the announcements section.
2. Read any current announcements posted by your essentials bank.

### Expected Result

You see all active, non-expired broadcast announcements from your bank, keeping you informed about policies, deadlines, or closures.

---

## Variations

- **Request types vary by bank**: Your essentials bank configures which request types are available. You may see only one type or multiple types.
- **Custom request units**: If your bank uses the packs feature, you may be able to request in custom units (e.g., packs) rather than individual item counts.
- **Required profile fields**: Your bank may require you to fill in certain profile fields for approval or recertification.
- **Family-based requests**: Only available if your bank enables this request type. You must have family and children records set up first.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| You cannot log in to the Partner Portal. | Your password may be incorrect, or your account may not be activated. | Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset your password. If your account is not yet activated, contact your essentials bank. |
| You do not see any request types available. | Your essentials bank may not have enabled any request types, or your account is not yet approved. | Contact your essentials bank to confirm your account is approved and that request types are configured. |
| Your request has been in "Pending" status for a long time. | The bank may not have started fulfillment yet. | Contact your essentials bank to follow up on the request. |
| You cannot add families or children. | Your bank may not use child/family-based requests, so these sections may not be available. | This is expected behavior if your bank only uses quantity-based or individual requests. |
| An item you need is not in the request dropdown. | The item may not be available to your partner group, or it has been deactivated by the bank. | Contact your essentials bank to ask about item availability. |
