# Broadcast Announcements User Manual

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to Human Essentials as an organization admin.
- You must have at least one partner agency to receive announcements.

## Overview

Broadcast Announcements let you send messages to all your partner agencies at once. Announcements appear on the partner portal dashboard when partners log in. You can set an optional expiration date so that announcements automatically stop displaying after a certain date. This is useful for communicating policy changes, deadlines, closures, or other important information to all partners simultaneously.

---

## Task 1: View All Broadcast Announcements

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Partner Agencies** to expand the menu.
2. Click **Partner Announcement**.
3. The page displays a list of all broadcast announcements for your organization.

### Expected Result

You see a table of announcements showing the title, message, expiration date (if set), and available actions.

---

## Task 2: Create a New Broadcast Announcement

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Broadcast Announcements** page by clicking **Partner Agencies** > **Partner Announcement** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **New Announcement** button.
3. In the **Title** field, enter a short, descriptive title for your announcement.
4. In the **Message** field, enter the full text of your announcement.
5. (Optional) In the **Expiry** field, select a date after which the announcement should no longer appear to partners.
6. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The announcement is created and immediately visible to all partner agencies on their partner portal dashboard. If you set an expiry date, the announcement will automatically stop displaying after that date.

---

## Task 3: Edit a Broadcast Announcement

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Broadcast Announcements** page.
2. Find the announcement you want to edit in the list.
3. Click the **Edit** button for that announcement.
4. Update the **Title**, **Message**, or **Expiry** fields as needed.
5. Click the **Save** button.

### Expected Result

The announcement is updated and partners will see the revised content on their dashboard.

---

## Task 4: Delete a Broadcast Announcement

### Steps

1. Navigate to the **Broadcast Announcements** page.
2. Find the announcement you want to remove.
3. Click the **Delete** button for that announcement.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

### Expected Result

The announcement is removed and no longer appears on partner dashboards.

---

## Variations

- **No expiry date**: If you leave the expiry date blank, the announcement will remain visible to partners indefinitely until you manually delete it.
- **Past expiry date**: Announcements with an expiry date in the past will not appear on partner dashboards but remain in your announcement list for reference.
- **Multiple announcements**: You can have multiple active announcements at the same time. All non-expired announcements appear on partner dashboards.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Partners say they cannot see your announcement. | The announcement may have an expiry date that has already passed. | Edit the announcement and either remove the expiry date or set it to a future date. |
| You do not see the **New Announcement** button. | You may not have organization admin permissions. | Contact your organization admin to either grant you admin access or create the announcement on your behalf. |
| The announcement text appears cut off on the partner portal. | The message may be very long and the partner portal displays a truncated view. | Consider shortening the message or breaking it into multiple announcements for clarity. |
