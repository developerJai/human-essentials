# Requesting a Human Essentials Account

Human Essentials is a free inventory management system for essentials banks (diaper banks and period-supply banks). Before your organization can start using it, you submit an account request, confirm it by email, and the Human Essentials team reviews it. This article explains how that process works and answers the most common questions.

## Who should request an account?

The request form is only for **essentials banks** — organizations that distribute diapers or period supplies to partner agencies, not directly to the public.

If you are a **partner agency** (you receive supplies from a bank and hand them to families), you do not request an account here. Your essentials bank is the only one who can create your account, so contact your local bank. If you already have a partner account, simply sign in. To find a bank near you, use the NDBN member directory (diapers) or the Alliance for Period Supplies directory (period supplies).

## How the process works

1. **Fill in the request form.** On the account request page, choose "I am an Essentials Bank", then provide:
   - Your name and email address
   - Your organization's name
   - Your organization's website (optional — if given, it must be a full address like `https://www.example.com`)
   - Your NDBN membership (optional, chosen from a list)
   - Request details — a short description (at least 50 characters) of your organization and how you would use Human Essentials
   - Complete the captcha check
2. **Watch your inbox.** After submitting you'll see a "Request Received!" page, and a confirmation email with the subject "[Action Required] Human Essential Account Request" is sent to the address you entered. Check your spam folder for mail from **no-reply@humanessentials.app**.
3. **Confirm your request.** The email contains a confirmation link. Click it and then the "I'm ready! Let's go!" button. Once confirmed, you'll see "We will be processing your request now."
4. **Wait for review.** The Human Essentials team is notified the moment you confirm. When they process your request they either create your organization and email you an invitation, or decline the request with an explanation.

## Try before you commit

The confirmation email also includes everything you need to evaluate the product first:

- Human Essentials is **100% free** — it's supported by the non-profit Code for Good, and Microsoft sponsors the servers.
- A link to the **Human Essentials User Guide**, with guidance on whether the system is right for you.
- **Demo site logins** (bank and partner roles) so you can explore a sandbox copy of the app. All demo data resets every day at 6 AM EST, and you should never enter real or sensitive information there.
- A series of eight getting-started videos (no longer updated, but still helpful).

## Common questions

**Why was my email address refused on the form?**
Each email address can be used for only one account request, and it can't already belong to an existing Human Essentials organization or user. Use a different address, or sign in if you already have an account.

**Why does my confirmation link say the request is "invalid, confirmed already, or processed already"?**
The link works exactly once. You'll see this message if you've already confirmed, if your account has already been set up, or if the link is broken or incomplete. If you believe your request is still pending, reach out to the team.

**I didn't get the confirmation email.**
Check your spam filters for mail from no-reply@humanessentials.app. If it isn't there, you may have mistyped your address — submit a new request with the correct email.

**What does "Request Details (min 50 characters)" mean?**
The description of your organization must be at least 50 characters long. Tell the team what your organization does and how you plan to use Human Essentials.

**What happens if my request is rejected?**
You'll receive an email with the subject "Human Essential Account Request Rejected" that includes the reason for the decision. You're welcome to reply with questions.

**How long does approval take?**
Requests are reviewed by a human after you confirm. You'll get your invitation email as soon as the team processes your request; they'll also email you if they have questions.

**Is there any cost?**
No. Human Essentials is completely free for essentials banks.

## Good to know

- The website field is optional, but if you fill it in, it must look like a full URL (for example `https://www.example.com`).
- Submitting the form does not create your account — the account is created only after you confirm and the team approves your request.
- If you ignore the confirmation email, your request simply stays pending; the team may eventually close it administratively.
