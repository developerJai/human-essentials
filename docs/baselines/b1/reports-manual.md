# Reports — Operations Guide

This guide is for operations staff of a diaper/essentials bank using Human Essentials. It covers the day-to-day summary reports and the year-end NDBN Annual Report.

## Before you start

- You need a staff login for your organization. All reports show only your organization's data.
- Summary reports depend on a date range: set the range on the report card's filter before reading the numbers.

## Viewing summary reports

### Donations summary

1. Open the **Donations (All Sources)** report.
2. Set the date range in the card's filter.
3. Read the headline: total items received and total money raised in the period.
4. Scroll to **Recent Donations** to see each donation's item count, its source, and how long ago it arrived. Click a donation to open it.
5. Use **New Donation** to record a donation without leaving the page, or **See more...** to go to the full donations list.

### Manufacturer donations

1. Open the **Manufacturer Donations** report and set your range.
2. The headline shows total items donated and by how many manufacturers.
3. **Recent Manufacturer Donations** lists up to ten manufacturers with their donation counts; click one to open its record.
4. **See more...** takes you to the donations list already filtered to manufacturer donations.

### Purchases summary

1. Open the **Purchases** report and set your range.
2. The headline shows total money spent. Below it are four category totals: diapers, period supplies, adult incontinence, and other — followed by total items purchased.
3. **Recent purchases** lists the latest purchases with item counts and vendor; **New Purchase** starts a new record.

### Product drives

1. Open the **Product Drives** report and set your range.
2. Read: number of drives active, items received through drives, and money raised.
3. **Recent Donations from Product Drives** lists the latest drive donations with the drive or participant name.

### Distributions summary

1. Open the **Distributions** report and set your range.
2. The headline shows items distributed in the period and, below it, items scheduled for future distribution.
3. **Recent distributions** lists each distribution with its partner and how long ago it was issued. **New Distribution** starts a new one.

### Itemized reports (donations, distributions, requests)

1. Open the itemized report you need and set your range.
2. Each row is one item: the quantity donated/distributed/requested in the period, and your current on-hand total.
3. **A red on-hand cell means that item is below your minimum** — plan a purchase or transfer.
4. In **Itemized Requests**, the item's unit (if your bank uses custom units) appears next to its name, and items you don't stock show 0 on hand.
5. If nothing matches the range, the card shows a "No ... found for the selected date range" notice.

### Activity graph

1. Open **Activity Graph** and set your range.
2. The bar chart compares three totals for the period: items received as donations, items purchased, and items distributed. Use it for a quick in-versus-out check.

## Working with the NDBN Annual Report

### Opening a report

1. Go to **Reports** (breadcrumb: Home > Reports). The **NDBN Annual Reports** card lists one entry per completed year, starting from your organization's earliest reporting year.
2. The note at the top tells you when the current year's report becomes available (at year end — the page shows the months remaining).
3. Click a year to open it. The first open of a year takes a moment while figures are calculated; afterwards the stored report loads instantly.

### Reading the report

The report has eight titled sections, each a simple two-column table (measure and value): Diapers; Warehouse and Storage; Adult Incontinence; Period Supplies; Other Items; Partner Agencies and Service Area; Children Served; Year End Summary. Kit contents are included in the diaper, adult incontinence, and period supply figures. The Year End Summary shows percentage changes versus the previous year for donations, money donated, and disposable diaper donations.

### Recalculating after data corrections

1. Open the year's report.
2. Click **Recalculate Report** (top of the page).
3. You are returned to the report with the message "Recalculated annual report!" and refreshed figures.

Do this whenever you backdate or correct donations, purchases, distributions, partner profiles, or storage locations for that year.

### Exporting to CSV

1. Open the year's report.
2. Click **Export Report**.
3. A CSV named `NdbnAnnuals-<year>-<today's date>.csv` downloads. It contains every measure from all eight sections as columns with one row of values — ready for the NDBN survey or a spreadsheet.

## Troubleshooting

- **A year is missing from the list** — only completed years appear; the current year is added after December.
- **Numbers look stale** — annual reports are cached; use **Recalculate Report**.
- **Zero or empty summary report** — check the date-range filter; it may be set to a period with no activity.
- **Square footage line mentions missing locations** — some storage locations have no square footage entered; update them under storage locations and recalculate.
