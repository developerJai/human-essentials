# Distributions — Knowledge Base Article

## What are distributions?

A distribution is how supplies leave your essentials bank and reach the community. Each distribution records a set of items going from one of your storage locations to one partner organization on a scheduled date. Distributions keep your inventory accurate, give your partners clear paperwork, and feed your reports.

## What can I do with distributions?

- Create a distribution from scratch, from a partner's request, or starting from a donation's contents.
- Schedule each distribution for a date and time, and choose how it gets to the partner: pick up, delivery, or shipped (with a shipping cost for shipped ones).
- Automatically email the partner when a distribution is created or changed, with a PDF packing slip attached.
- Send an automatic reminder email the day before the scheduled date.
- See all distributions on a calendar, subscribe to that calendar from your own calendar app, and view a simple "what's going out today" pickup list.
- Mark a distribution as complete once it's been picked up or delivered.
- Correct mistakes after the fact, or "reclaim" a distribution entirely to put the items back into inventory.
- Export everything to spreadsheets, including an itemized breakdown and an estimated by-county view.

## How does a distribution work, start to finish?

1. **Create it.** Choose the partner, the storage location the items come from, the date and time, the delivery method, and the items with quantities. If you start from a partner request, the partner and requested items are pre-filled for you (the scheduled date defaults to the next day). Before anything is saved, a confirmation window shows exactly which items and quantities you're about to distribute, and asks you to confirm.
2. **Inventory is reduced.** As soon as the distribution is saved, the items are deducted from the chosen storage location. If you ask for more of an item than is on hand, the distribution won't save and the problem items are pointed out. You'll also get a warning if a distribution drops any item below your minimum or recommended levels.
3. **The partner is notified.** If the partner has reminders enabled, they automatically receive an email with the details and a PDF attachment. If you enabled the day-before reminder, a reminder email goes out one day before the scheduled date.
4. **It gets picked up or delivered.** A distribution starts in **Scheduled** status. When it's been handed over, mark it complete and the status changes to **Complete**.
5. **Fix or undo if needed.** You can edit a distribution to correct it — the inventory adjusts automatically and the partner can be re-notified of the changes. Reclaiming a distribution deletes it and returns all of its items to inventory.

## Common questions

**Q: Why can't I pick the storage location I want when creating a distribution?**
A: Only active storage locations that currently have inventory appear in the list when creating a distribution.

**Q: Why won't my distribution save?**
A: The most common reason is requesting more of an item than the storage location has on hand. The error message tells you which items are short. A distribution also needs at least one item with a quantity of 1 or more, and a delivery method.

**Q: Who gets the distribution emails?**
A: The email goes to the person who made the request when the distribution came from a partner request; otherwise it goes to the partner's main email. The partner email is CC'd, and for pick-up distributions any extra pick-up contacts in the partner's profile are CC'd too. Partners who have notifications turned off don't get emails.

**Q: Can I edit a distribution after its date has passed?**
A: Only organization administrators can edit past or completed distributions. Other users can edit a distribution only while it is scheduled for a future date. Be careful with past edits — the record should match what was actually given out.

**Q: Why is the Edit button disabled on some distributions?**
A: Distributions containing items that have since been made inactive can't be edited or reclaimed until those items are reactivated.

**Q: What happens when I reclaim a distribution?**
A: The distribution is deleted and every item in it goes back into the storage location it came from.

**Q: What does the shipping cost field do?**
A: It records the cost of shipping and is only kept for distributions whose delivery method is "Shipped" — for pick up or delivery it is cleared automatically.

**Q: Can partners see a calendar of their pickups?**
A: Your team can copy a private calendar link from the distribution schedule page and add it to Google Calendar or similar apps. It shows pickups from the last year onward with partner names and locations.

**Q: Why did I get a warning about an audit when editing?**
A: If your team audited the storage location after the distribution was created, correcting a typo is fine, but if the physical amounts changed you'll also need to make an inventory adjustment.

## Tips & best practices

- Review the confirmation window carefully before saving — it's your last check before inventory moves.
- Use the day-before reminder checkbox so partners don't miss pickups.
- Use the pickup-day view each morning to see everything going out that day and the total items to stage.
- Export the itemized breakdown when you need to compare what went out against what's still on hand.

## Limitations

- A distribution comes from a single storage location and goes to a single partner — you can't split one distribution across locations or partners.
- Very old distributions can't be deleted or have their contents changed once a newer inventory snapshot exists; you'll be told the cutoff date if you try.
- Reports that estimate distributions by county are proportional estimates based on each partner's service area, not exact counts.
