# Reports in Human Essentials

Human Essentials gives your bank two kinds of reporting: **everyday summary reports** you can filter by date, and the official **NDBN Annual Report** that is assembled for you at the end of each year.

## Everyday summary reports

Each summary report is a card you can filter by a date range. Pick the dates you care about and the numbers update for just that period.

- **Donations (All Sources)** — how many items you received and how much money was raised in the period, plus a list of your most recent donations showing where each came from and how long ago it arrived. You can start a new donation right from this page.
- **Manufacturer Donations** — items donated by manufacturers in the period, how many different manufacturers gave, and your ten most recent manufacturer donors with their donation counts.
- **Purchases** — total money spent in the period, broken down into diapers, period supplies, adult incontinence supplies, and other, plus total items bought and your most recent purchases.
- **Product Drives** — how many drives were active, how many items came in through drives, and how much money they raised, with the latest drive donations listed.
- **Distributions** — total items distributed in the period, how many items are scheduled for future distributions, and your most recent distributions with the partner each one went to.
- **Itemized Donations / Itemized Distributions / Itemized Requests** — item-by-item tables showing, for each product, how much was donated (or distributed, or requested) and how much you currently have on hand. If your on-hand amount for an item has fallen below your minimum, the row is highlighted in red so you can spot shortages at a glance.
- **Activity Graph** — a simple bar chart comparing items received through donations, items purchased, and items distributed over the chosen period.

If there is no activity for the dates you selected, the report tells you so instead of showing an empty table.

## The NDBN Annual Report

At the end of every year, Human Essentials compiles a complete annual report for your organization — the figures commonly needed for NDBN annual surveys. The Reports page lists one report per completed year, going back to your organization's earliest reporting year. The current year's report becomes available once the year ends; the page shows how many months remain.

Each annual report contains eight sections:

1. **Diapers** — disposable and cloth diapers distributed (including diapers inside kits), monthly averages, product drive results (in-person and virtual), how much was donated versus purchased, money spent on diapers, and where you purchased from.
2. **Warehouse and Storage** — how many storage locations you have, their combined square footage, and your largest storage site type.
3. **Adult Incontinence** — supplies distributed, adults assisted per month, supplies per adult, and the donated/purchased split.
4. **Period Supplies** — period supplies distributed (including those in kits), the types you stock, and the donated/purchased split.
5. **Other Items** — everything outside the categories above: quantities, split, spend, and a product list.
6. **Partner Agencies and Service Area** — how many partner agencies you serve, a count per agency type, and the zip codes you cover.
7. **Children Served** — total and average monthly children served, and whether you repackage diapers or distribute monthly.
8. **Year End Summary** — how this year compares with last year: percentage change in donations, in money donated, and in disposable diaper donations.

### Working with an annual report

- **It's saved for you.** The first time you open a year's report, the numbers are calculated and stored, so it loads instantly afterwards.
- **Recalculate when data changes.** If you've corrected past donations or distributions, click **Recalculate Report** to refresh the stored figures. You'll see a confirmation that the report was recalculated.
- **Export it.** Click **Export Report** to download the whole report as a CSV spreadsheet; the file is named with the report year and today's date.

## Common questions

**Q: Why don't I see this year in the annual report list?**
A: Annual reports cover completed years only. The list shows every full year since your organization started reporting; the current year appears after it ends.

**Q: I fixed some old records but the annual report still shows the old numbers. Why?**
A: Annual reports are stored once calculated. Open the year and click **Recalculate Report** to refresh it.

**Q: What does the red highlighting in the itemized reports mean?**
A: That item's current on-hand quantity is below the minimum you've set — a signal to restock.

**Q: Do kit contents count in the annual report?**
A: Yes. Diapers, adult incontinence supplies, and period supplies packed inside kits are counted alongside loose items.

**Q: Can I change the dates on a summary report?**
A: Yes — every summary card has a date-range filter; the numbers and recent-activity lists update to match.
