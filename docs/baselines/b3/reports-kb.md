# Reports

> A comprehensive guide to Reports.

---

## What is Reports?

Reports is where your bank sees its numbers. It gives you everyday summary pages — what came in, what went out, what you spent, and what's running low — filtered to any date range you choose, plus an official year-end report (the NDBN Annual Report) that the system assembles for you from a full year of activity.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Filter donation, purchase, distribution, product drive, and manufacturer summaries to any date range
- See item-by-item tables of what was donated, distributed, or requested, next to what you currently have on hand
- Spot shortages instantly — items below your minimum are highlighted in red
- Compare items received, purchased, and distributed on one bar chart
- Open a complete annual report for any completed year, back to your organization's first reporting year
- Refresh an annual report's figures after correcting past records
- Download an annual report as a CSV spreadsheet for the NDBN survey

---

## How It Works

**Everyday summaries** are always live:

1. **Pick a report** — for example Donations (All Sources), Purchases, or Distributions.
2. **Set the date range** — the card's filter controls every number on it.
3. **Read the headlines** — items received, money raised, money spent, or items distributed for that period.
4. **Drill in** — recent-activity lists link straight to each donation, purchase, or distribution, and "See more..." opens the full list.

**The NDBN Annual Report** follows a yearly cycle:

1. **During the year** — the Reports page shows how many months remain until the current year's report becomes available.
2. **After year end** — the year appears in the list. Opening it the first time calculates all figures and saves them.
3. **Review** — the report presents eight sections: Diapers; Warehouse and Storage; Adult Incontinence; Period Supplies; Other Items; Partner Agencies and Service Area; Children Served; and a Year End Summary comparing the year with the one before.
4. **Refresh if needed** — if you've corrected past records, recalculate the report to update the stored figures.
5. **Export** — download the whole report as a spreadsheet.

## Common Questions

**Q: Why isn't the current year in the annual report list?**
A: Annual reports cover completed years only. The Reports page tells you how many months remain before this year's report becomes available.

**Q: I corrected some old donations, but the annual report hasn't changed. Why?**
A: Annual reports are saved after the first calculation so they load quickly. Open the year and use **Recalculate Report** — you'll see a confirmation and updated figures.

**Q: What does a red row in an itemized report mean?**
A: Your current on-hand amount for that item is below the minimum you've set. It's a restock signal.

**Q: Are items packed inside kits counted?**
A: Yes. Diapers, adult incontinence supplies, and period supplies inside distributed kits are counted along with loose items in the annual report.

**Q: What exactly is in the Year End Summary?**
A: Three comparisons with the previous year, shown as percentages: change in the number of donations, change in total money donated, and change in disposable diaper donations.

**Q: What file do I get when I export?**
A: A CSV spreadsheet named with the report year and the download date, containing every measure from all eight sections.

**Q: Can other organizations see my reports?**
A: No. Every report — daily or annual — shows only your own organization's data.

**Q: What happens if there's no activity in my chosen date range?**
A: The report says so plainly (for example, "No distributions found for the selected date range") instead of showing an empty table.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Recalculate the annual report after any end-of-year data cleanup, and only then export — the export reflects the saved figures.
- Use the itemized reports weekly: the red highlighting is the fastest way to catch items slipping below minimums.
- The Activity Graph is a quick health check: received and purchased bars should comfortably cover the distributed bar over time.
- Keep storage-location square footage up to date — the warehouse section of the annual report notes how many locations are missing it.
- Keep partner profiles complete; agency counts and zip-codes-served in the annual report come from them.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Annual reports exist only for completed years — you can't generate a partial-year report.
- Annual report figures are frozen once calculated; they change only when you recalculate.
- Product-drive money totals in the Diapers section are shown in whole dollars.
- The adults-assisted and children-served figures are estimates derived from distribution quantities and each item's typical distribution amount.
- Summary pages always reflect the current state of your records — if someone edits a donation, the summary changes immediately, but the saved annual report does not.

---

## Related Features

- **Donations** — every received item and dollar in these reports starts as a donation record.
- **Purchases** — spending summaries and the money-spent lines of the annual report come from purchase records.
- **Distributions** — outgoing totals, itemized distribution tables, and served-population figures come from distributions.
- **Product Drives** — drive counts and drive fundraising totals feed both the drive summary and the annual Diapers section.
- **Kits** — kit contents are unpacked into the annual diaper, adult incontinence, and period supply counts.
- **Partners** — partner profiles supply the agency types and service-area zip codes in the annual report.
