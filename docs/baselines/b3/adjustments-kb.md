# Inventory Adjustments

> A comprehensive guide to Inventory Adjustments.

---

## What is Inventory Adjustments?

Inventory Adjustments let your bank fix the recorded quantity of supplies at a storage location when it doesn't match what's physically there. After a recount, damage, loss, or a data-entry slip, you create an adjustment that adds or removes the difference — with a comment explaining why — and your inventory totals update right away.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Correct several items at once in a single adjustment, mixing additions and removals
- Add inventory with a plain number, remove it with a negative number (minus sign)
- Browse your organization's full adjustment history, newest first
- Filter the history by storage location, by the person who made the adjustment, and by date range
- Open any adjustment to see exactly what was added or removed
- Download the filtered history as a spreadsheet with **Export Adjustments**
- Scan barcodes to enter items faster

---

## How It Works

1. **Open the adjustments page** — from your dashboard, go to Inventory Adjustments. You'll see every adjustment your organization has made, with its date, storage location, comment, and a summary of how many changes it contained.
2. **Start a new adjustment** — click **New Adjustment**, pick the storage location that needs correcting, and write a brief comment explaining the reason.
3. **List the item changes** — add each item and its quantity: a plain number adds that many, a number with a minus sign removes that many. You can keep adding items, and one adjustment can both add some items and remove others.
4. **Submit** — the system checks your entries. If a removal is bigger than what's on hand, or the location isn't yours, the adjustment is rejected with a clear message and nothing is saved.
5. **Done** — on success you see "Adjustment was successful." and the adjustment's detail page. Inventory totals at that location update immediately, and the adjustment becomes a permanent part of your history.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I edit or delete an adjustment after saving it?**
A: No. Adjustments are permanent to protect your audit trail. If you made a mistake, create a new adjustment with the opposite quantities to reverse it, and mention the original in the comment.

**Q: What happens if I try to remove more of an item than the location has?**
A: The entire adjustment is rejected with an error message, and nothing is saved. Reduce the removal to at most the quantity on hand.

**Q: Can one adjustment cover two storage locations?**
A: No — each adjustment applies to exactly one storage location. If stock moved between locations, use a Transfer instead of adjustments.

**Q: Why should I use an adjustment instead of recording a donation or distribution?**
A: Donations and distributions represent real supply movements with your community and partners. Adjustments are purely corrections — they bring the system in line with reality without inventing a transaction.

**Q: What if I add the same item twice in one adjustment?**
A: The duplicate lines are combined automatically into a single line before saving.

**Q: Can I adjust kits?**
A: No. Only individual (loose) items appear in the item picker. Kit quantities are managed through the Kits feature.

**Q: Who can see my adjustments?**
A: Only people signed in to your organization. Each adjustment also records who created it, and the history can be filtered by that person.

**Q: Is there a report of all adjustments?**
A: Yes — filter the list however you like and click **Export Adjustments** to download a spreadsheet. It includes the date, storage area, comment, number of updates, and a column for every item your organization carries.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Write comments that your future self can act on — "Oct recount: 12 extra size 3 found behind pallet" beats "fix".
- Do a quick physical check of the location's current stock before entering removals, so the adjustment isn't rejected for exceeding what's on hand.
- Group all corrections for one location into a single adjustment rather than many small ones — the history stays readable and each item appears once.
- Use the date-range and location filters before exporting, so the spreadsheet contains only the period you're reconciling.
- To undo a mistake, reverse it with an opposite adjustment and reference the original in the comment — that way both records tell the full story.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Adjustments cannot be changed or removed once saved — corrections are made by new, reversing adjustments.
- One adjustment covers one storage location only.
- Removals cannot exceed the recorded quantity on hand; if any line fails, the whole adjustment fails.
- Only loose items can be adjusted — kits are excluded.
- The **Export Adjustments** button only appears when at least one adjustment matches your current filters.
- The storage location must belong to your organization and be active.

## Related Features

- **Storage Locations** — adjustments change the inventory recorded at a storage location.
- **Transfers** — use these to move stock between two locations; adjustments only correct counts in place.
- **Audits** — a formal process to verify physical counts, which may lead you to make adjustments.
- **Items** — the catalog of loose items available in an adjustment.
