# Account Requests

> A comprehensive guide to Account Requests.

---

## What is Account Requests?

Account Requests is how a new essentials bank (a diaper bank or period-supply bank) applies for a free Human Essentials account. You fill in a short application about your organization, confirm it from an email, and the Human Essentials team reviews it — approving your new account or letting you know why they couldn't.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Apply for an account online without needing a login
- Prove your email address with a one-click confirmation link
- Optionally identify your organization by its NDBN membership
- Try a free demo site (login details arrive in your confirmation email) before you commit
- Receive a clear emailed explanation if your application is declined

---

## How It Works

1. **You apply** — On the account request form, you first say whether you're an *Essentials Bank* or a *Partner Agency*. Only banks apply here; partner agencies get their accounts from their bank. As a bank, you enter your name, email, organization name, an optional website, your NDBN membership (optional), and at least 50 characters describing how you'd use Human Essentials. A quick captcha check keeps spam out.
2. **You see "Request Received!"** — and a confirmation email (subject "[Action Required] Human Essential Account Request") is sent to your address from no-reply@humanessentials.app.
3. **You confirm** — The email contains a confirmation link. Click it, then click "I'm ready! Let's go!". You'll see "Confirmed! We will be processing your request now."
4. **The team reviews** — Confirming notifies the Human Essentials team right away. They either set up your organization and email your invitation, or send a rejection email explaining their reason. They may also email you questions first.

> Your request moves through stages: **Started** → **Confirmed by you** → **Approved** (account created) or **Declined**

## Common Questions

**Q: Is Human Essentials really free?**
A: Yes — 100% free. It's supported by the non-profit Code for Good, and Microsoft sponsors the servers.

**Q: I'm a partner agency that hands supplies directly to families. Can I apply here?**
A: No — partner accounts are created by the essentials bank you partner with, so contact your local bank. If you already have a partner account, just sign in. To find a bank near you, use the NDBN member directory (diapers) or the Alliance for Period Supplies directory (period supplies).

**Q: I never got the confirmation email. What now?**
A: Check your spam folder for mail from **no-reply@humanessentials.app**. If it's not there, your email address may have been mistyped — submit a new request with the correct address.

**Q: Why does the form reject my email address?**
A: Each email can only be used once, and it can't already belong to an existing Human Essentials organization or user. Use a different address, or sign in if you already have an account.

**Q: Why won't my confirmation link work anymore?**
A: The link works once. If you've already confirmed, or your account has already been created, you'll see a page saying the request is "invalid, confirmed already, or processed already". If you're still waiting, your invitation will come by email.

**Q: What should I write in "Request Details"?**
A: At least 50 characters about your organization and how you'd use Human Essentials — who you serve, what supplies you distribute, roughly how you operate.

**Q: Can I try the app before applying or confirming?**
A: Yes. Your confirmation email includes demo-site logins for both a bank user and a partner user. The demo resets all data every day at 6 AM EST, and other people can see what you enter there — so never use real client information.

**Q: What happens if my request is declined?**
A: You'll receive an email with the subject "Human Essential Account Request Rejected" that includes the team's reason. You can reach out if you have questions.

**Q: Does my website address have to be in a special format?**
A: It's optional, but if you provide it, it must be a full web address like `https://www.example.com`.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Write generous Request Details — this is what the review team reads to understand your organization, and the form requires at least 50 characters anyway.
- Confirm promptly: the team isn't notified about your request until you click the confirmation link.
- Explore the demo site and the Human Essentials User Guide (both linked in your confirmation email) before your bank goes live — the guide helps you decide whether the system fits and what to prepare. Eight getting-started videos are also linked, though they're no longer kept up to date.
- Add no-reply@humanessentials.app to your safe-senders list so the confirmation and invitation emails don't land in spam.
- If you're an NDBN member, select your membership on the form — it helps the team verify your organization.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Submitting the form does not create an account — approval is a manual review that happens only after you confirm.
- The confirmation link is single-use; once confirmed or processed, it stops working.
- The form is only for essentials banks, not partner agencies or individuals seeking supplies.
- If the team closes your request administratively (for example, a duplicate), you will not receive a notification email — only rejections are emailed.
- The request form on the demo/staging site is disabled; real applications are only accepted on the live Human Essentials website.

## Related Features

- **Organizations** — your approved request becomes your bank's organization account on Human Essentials.
- **Partner management** — once your bank is live, you create accounts for your partner agencies (they never apply through this form).
- **Demo / sandbox site** — a full copy of the app for exploring safely, using the credentials from your confirmation email.
