# Account Requests

> A comprehensive guide to Account Requests.

---

## What is Account Requests?

Account Requests is how a new essentials bank applies for a Human Essentials account. You fill in a public application form, confirm your request from a link emailed to you, and the Human Essentials team reviews it — creating your organization if approved, or emailing you the reason if declined.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Apply for an account from a public form — no login needed, just a quick captcha check
- Identify yourself as an essentials bank or a partner agency so you're routed correctly
- Optionally select your NDBN membership on the application
- Prove you own your email address with a one-click confirmation link
- Receive the review team's reason by email if your application is rejected

---

## How It Works

1. **You apply** — On the Essentials Bank Account Request Form you first choose between "I am an Essentials Bank" and "I am a Partner Agency". Banks fill in the form: your name, email, organization name, an optional website, an optional NDBN membership, and request details of at least 50 characters. A captcha must be completed before submitting.
2. **You see "Request Received!"** — Your application is saved and a confirmation email is sent to you from no-reply@humanessentials.app (check your spam filters).
3. **You confirm** — The email contains a special confirmation link. It takes you to a page asking "Are you ready to get started?"; click "I'm ready! Let's go!" and you'll see "Confirmed! We will be processing your request now."
4. **The team reviews** — Confirming notifies the Human Essentials team at info@humanessentials.app. They either create your organization (your invitation is promised by email), reject your request with an emailed reason, or close it administratively.

> Requests move through stages: **Started** → **Confirmed by you** → **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Closed by the team**

## Common Questions

**Q: I'm a partner agency — can I use this form?**
A: No. Partner agency accounts are created by their essentials banks, not through this form. Choose the "I am a Partner Agency" option on the form and you'll be routed accordingly instead of applying.

**Q: Why does the form reject my email address?**
A: Your email must not already be used by another account request, an existing organization, or an existing user. Use a different address, or sign in with your existing account.

**Q: How long do the request details need to be?**
A: At least 50 characters. Use them to describe your organization — this is what the review team reads.

**Q: Is the website field required?**
A: No, it's optional — but if you fill it in, it must be a full address that looks like `https://www.example.com`.

**Q: My confirmation link shows "The account request is either invalid, confirmed already, or processed already." Why?**
A: Confirmation links are effectively single-use. You'll see this if you already confirmed, if your organization has already been created, or if the link was truncated when copying. If your request is genuinely still pending, copy the complete link from the email and try again.

**Q: Does my confirmation link expire?**
A: No — it keeps working until you confirm or your account is set up, after which it stops working.

**Q: What happens if my request is rejected?**
A: The team must give a reason when rejecting, and that reason is emailed to you word for word.

**Q: What does it mean if my request was "closed"?**
A: The team can also close a request administratively. A reason is recorded internally, but no email is sent — so you won't be notified of a closure, unlike a rejection.

**Q: Can I apply again after being rejected or closed?**
A: Yes. Once a request is rejected or closed, its email address is freed up and can be used to apply again.

**Q: I submitted on the demo/staging site and nothing happened.**
A: The request form on the staging site is disabled — it only points you to the production form on the live Human Essentials website. There is a sandbox site for exploring the app, but its data resets daily at 6 AM EST.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Write meaningful request details (minimum 50 characters) — the review team sees exactly what you type.
- Confirm promptly: the team is only notified about your request after you click the confirmation link.
- Watch your spam folder for mail from no-reply@humanessentials.app — both the confirmation and later correspondence come from there.
- Copy the entire confirmation link from the email if clicking doesn't work; truncated links are a common cause of the "invalid" page.
- Use an email address that isn't already attached to any Human Essentials organization or user.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Submitting the form does not create an account. Your organization is created only after you confirm and the team approves the request.
- The confirmation link stops working once the request is confirmed or processed — it cannot be reused.
- Only essentials banks may apply; partner agencies get accounts from their banks.
- If the team closes (rather than rejects) your request, you will not receive any notification email.
- Every change to your request is kept in an audit history.

---

## Related Features

- **Organizations** — an approved request becomes your bank's organization; the team creates it directly from your confirmed request.
- **NDBN membership directory** — the application form lets you select your NDBN membership from a list.
- **Users & sign-in** — your email is checked against existing users, and the approved bank's administrator receives an invitation email to set up access.
