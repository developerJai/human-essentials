# Reports

> A comprehensive guide to Reports.

---

## What is Reports?

Reports is where your bank sees what's coming in, what's going out, and how the year adds up. It offers nine everyday summary pages — covering donations, manufacturer donations, purchases, product drives, distributions, item-by-item breakdowns, and an activity chart — each filtered to any date range you choose, plus the official NDBN Annual Report compiled for every completed year.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Filter summaries of donations, manufacturer donations, purchases, product drives, and distributions to any date range, each with a recent-activity list
- Start a new donation, purchase, or distribution straight from the matching report page
- Review item-by-item tables — donated, distributed, or requested amounts next to what you have on hand — with items below your minimum highlighted in red
- Compare items received, purchased, and distributed on a single bar chart (the Activity Graph)
- Open an annual report for any completed year, back to your organization's earliest reporting year
- Refresh an annual report's figures with one click after correcting past records
- Export any annual report as a CSV spreadsheet

---

## How It Works

**Everyday summaries** always reflect your current records:

1. **Choose a report** — for example Donations (All Sources), Purchases, Distributions, Product Drives, or Manufacturer Donations.
2. **Set the date range** — every figure on the page follows the range you pick.
3. **Read the results** — headline totals at the top, a list of recent activity beneath, and a "See more..." link to the full records.
4. **Act on what you see** — quick-create buttons (**New Donation**, **New Purchase**, **New Distribution**) let you record activity without leaving the report.

**The NDBN Annual Report** follows a yearly rhythm:

1. **During the year** — the report list tells you the current year's report will be "available in N months".
2. **After year end** — the completed year joins the list. Opening it calculates eight sections of figures and saves them so later visits load instantly.
3. **Review the eight sections** — Diapers; Warehouse and Storage; Adult Incontinence; Period Supplies; Other Items; Partner Agencies and Service Area; Children Served; and the Year End Summary, which shows percentage change versus the previous year in donations, money raised, and disposable diaper donations.
4. **Recalculate when needed** — after correcting past records, refresh the report; you'll see the confirmation "Recalculated annual report!".
5. **Export** — download the full report as a CSV file named with the year and the date of export.

## Common Questions

**Q: Why isn't the current year in the annual report list?**
A: Annual reports are only produced for completed years. The list runs from your organization's earliest reporting year through last year, and tells you how many months remain before this year's report is available.

**Q: I fixed some past records, but the annual report still shows old numbers. Why?**
A: Annual report figures are saved when first calculated so the report opens quickly. Use **Recalculate Report** on the year's page to rebuild the figures from your current records.

**Q: What does a red cell in the itemized reports mean?**
A: That item's on-hand amount has fallen below the minimum you've set — a signal to restock. This highlighting appears in the itemized distributions and itemized requests tables.

**Q: Do items packed inside kits count in the annual report?**
A: Yes. Kit contents are counted into the diaper, adult incontinence, and period supply figures alongside loose items.

**Q: What's in the Year End Summary?**
A: Three year-over-year comparisons shown as percentages: change in donations, change in money raised, and change in disposable diaper donations. If the previous year had none, growth shows as +100%.

**Q: What do I get when I export an annual report?**
A: A CSV spreadsheet named with the report year and today's date, containing every measure from all eight sections — ready for the NDBN survey.

**Q: Can another organization see my reports?**
A: No. Every report is restricted to your own organization's data.

**Q: What happens if my date range has no activity?**
A: The report shows a clear message — for example, "No distributions found for the selected date range" — rather than an empty table.

**Q: How are "children served" and "adults assisted" calculated?**
A: They're estimates based on the quantities you distributed and the typical amount of product distributed per person, averaged across the twelve months of the year.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Recalculate an annual report before exporting it if you've made any data corrections — the export contains the saved figures.
- Scan the itemized reports regularly; red highlighting is the fastest early warning for low stock.
- Use the Activity Graph as a monthly health check: received plus purchased should keep pace with distributed.
- Keep storage-location square footage filled in — the Warehouse and Storage section flags locations missing it.
- Keep partner profiles complete, since agency types and zip codes served in the annual report come from them.
- Product-drive money in the Diapers section is reported in whole dollars, so small cent differences won't appear.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Annual reports cannot be produced mid-year; only completed years appear in the list.
- Annual figures are frozen once calculated and change only when you recalculate — daily summaries, by contrast, are always live.
- Monthly averages in the annual report always divide by twelve, so a bank that started mid-year will see understated monthly figures for its first year.
- Drive fundraising amounts are rounded down to whole dollars in the annual Diapers section.
- Serving estimates (children served, adults assisted per month) are derived figures, not head counts.

## Related Features

- **Donations** — the records behind donation summaries and the annual donation and diaper figures.
- **Purchases** — spending totals and the per-category money-spent lines.
- **Distributions** — outgoing totals, itemized breakdowns, and the served-population estimates.
- **Product Drives** — drive counts, items collected, and money raised, split between in-person and virtual drives.
- **Kits** — bundled items whose contents are counted in the annual figures.
- **Storage Locations** — the source of the warehouse count, square footage, and largest-site figures.
- **Partners** — partner profiles feed the agency counts, types, and zip codes served.
